Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the platform access my funds?
No. Your funds remain completely in your Binance account. You create an API key with trade authorization only and withdrawals disabled — the platform can place orders on your behalf but can never move money out of your account.
Does the bot decide its own strategy?
No. The bot is a tool that implements your rules. You set the buy threshold, sell threshold, purchase amount and market/limit choice — it executes your configuration with machine discipline, nothing more.
Does the bot keep working when my computer is off?
Yes. Bots run 24/7 on cloud servers, so they continue working while you sleep or your devices are offline. The mobile-compatible web panel lets you check in from anywhere.
How fast is the panel compared to the exchange?
The panel uses WebSocket technology rather than old-generation polling, so order fills and price movements on the exchange are reflected on your screen within milliseconds — almost one-to-one with Binance.
Can I run KRIPTRADER and KRIPUMP at the same time?
Yes. The two bots can run simultaneously on the same account with separate licenses — grid accumulation and pump tracking working side by side.
What happens if the central server goes down?
Your license is encrypted inside the bot itself, so even if there are problems on the central server, your bot's license continues to work and trading is not interrupted.
